Additional Services & What Each Extra Covers

Every add-on can be combined with your main cleaning visit so our team takes care of appliances, hard-to-reach interiors, windows, and laundry in one seamless appointment.

Oven Cleaning

+€35

We use safe, low-fume degreasers to break down baked-on carbon and grease inside the oven chamber, soak and scrub removable racks and grill pans, and leave both sides of the oven glass door sparkling.

Fridge Cleaning

+€18

A complete interior hygiene reset for your fridge: we remove and wash all glass shelves and salad crispers, wipe sticky spills, neutralize lingering food odours, and detail rubber door seals.

Inside Kitchen Cupboards

+€25

Ideal for spring resets or moving day. Our cleaners vacuum crumbs from empty or cleared cabinets, wipe down every shelf and drawer base, clean metal hinges, and polish the inside of cupboard doors.

Extractor Hood Cleaning

+€15

Cooking steam leaves stubborn oily film on cooker hoods. We remove and degrease the metal grease-trap filters, clean the underside canopy and light lenses, and polish the outer hood surface streak-free.

Microwave Cleaning (Inside)

+€5

We steam-soften dried food splatters inside your microwave so there is no scratching, then sanitize the ceiling, side walls, vent grille, door seal, and wash the glass rotating plate.

Stair Cleaning

+€20

High-traffic stairs gather dust quickly in corners and along spindles. We detail every tread and riser, wipe handrails and banisters, clean individual balusters, and dust stair-side skirting.

Window Cleaning

+€12 / win

Maximise natural daylight inside your home or shop. We wash and squeegee internal glass panes to remove fingerprints and condensation streaks, then clean uPVC/wood frames, handles, and sills.

Ironing Service

+€22 / hr

Free up your evenings by letting us handle the ironing basket right at your home. We carefully press shirts, school uniforms, trousers, and bed linen, leaving everything neatly hung or folded.

Extra Cleaning Hour(s)

+€22 / hr

Add dedicated extra time whenever a property has heavy build-up, stubborn grime, or neglected areas—ensuring our team never rushes and delivers thorough, spotless results.
